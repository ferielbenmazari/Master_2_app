## CHOOSING YOUR METHOD:

> "The selection of methods for a given research project depends on many local contingencies, including available resources, access to subjects, opportunity to control the variables of interest, and, of course, the skills of the researcher." *(Easterbrook et al., 2008)*

1. **What is studied:** Do you want to study single cases in-depth, or do you want to study statistics of samples?
2. **Where it is studied:** Laboratory vs. Field.
3. **How it is studied:** As an observer or interacting/intervening in the studied phenomena.
4. **Why it is studied:** Exploration, evaluation, comparison, understanding reasons, etc.
5. **Availability of resources / possible risks:** Time schedule, availability of people, equipment, confidentiality, validity threats.

## WHERE IS IT STUDIED?

- **Field study:**
  - Expensive
  - Population elements are heterogeneous, hard to control
  - Rich qualitative data (in-depth analysis is possible)
  - Hard to reproduce
- **"Lab" / "Desktop":**
  - Can fix/control the setting (influencing parameters)
  - Reproducible

*Irina Rychkova, CRI, University Paris 1*

---

## HOW IS IT STUDIED?

- **Observer (not participating in the phenomenon):**
  - Cannot influence the results.
  - *Retrospective case studies, Surveys.*
- **Active participant (interacting with phenomenon):**
  - Can influence the study results.
  - *Experiment, POC, Action research.*

*Irina Rychkova, CRI, University Paris 1*

## CONSTRAINTS TO FEASIBILITY

- **Data:** Restricted, unavailable in time, incomplete.
- **Sample size:** Too small, too big (processing time!), bias (demographic, convenience).
- **Resources:** Access to specific equipment/technology unavailable, insufficient processing power, high learning curve for complexity.
- **Time:** Results will not be obtained/validated within the project timeframe.

## METHOD SELECTION DECISION TREE 1 (ORGANIZATIONAL IMPACT)

1. **What do you study?**
   - **Impact of a single technology/method/change in my organisation:**
     - *In present (ongoing project):*
       - How many projects do you study?
         - One/two (e.g. pilot project) $\rightarrow$ **Case Study / Action Research / PoC**
         - Many $\rightarrow$ **Action Research**
     - *In the past (finished projects, results available):*
       - Qualitative or Quantitative analysis?
         - Quantitative $\rightarrow$ **Survey**
         - Qualitative $\rightarrow$ **Interviews**
   - **Comparison of multiple technologies/methods in my organisation:**
     - *In present:* Do you control some variables?
       - Yes $\rightarrow$ **Experiment**
       - No $\rightarrow$ **Action Research / Case Study**

*Irina Rychkova, CRI, University Paris 1*

---

## METHOD SELECTION DECISION TREE 2 (DATA COLLECTION)

1. **How do you collect your data?**
   - **By searching and analysing research literature:**
     - How specific is your RQ?
       - Broad, generic $\rightarrow$ **Systematic Mapping Study (SMS)**
       - Narrow, specific $\rightarrow$ Evidences/sources available?
         - Yes $\rightarrow$ **Systematic Literature Review (SLR)**
         - Not enough $\rightarrow$ Re-scope research
   - **By designing and running my own experiment:**
     - How many samples/runs do you have?
       - Many $\rightarrow$ **Experiment**
       - One $\rightarrow$ **Proof of Concept (POC)**

## DATA COLLECTION AND ANALYSIS METHODS

- **Collected data:**
  - Numerical (quantitative)
  - Text, sound, images, video (qualitative)
- **Collection methods:**
  - *Primary sources:* Interviews (open-ended, structured), Group discussions, Questionnaires, Observation studies, Simulation, Document study, Field study.
  - *Secondary sources:* Literature.

```
Research Strategy (Survey, Experiment, Case Study, Action Research)
       │
       ▼
Research Method
       ├─── Data Generation Method (Questionnaire, Interview, Observation, Documents)
       └─── Data Analysis Method
                 ├─── Qualitative Analysis Method (Content analysis, Narrative analysis)
                 └─── Quantitative Analysis Method (Statistical methods)
```
