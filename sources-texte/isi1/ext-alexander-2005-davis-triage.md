# Ian Alexander — review of Davis, Just Enough Requirements Management (extraits)

## Triage

This book does something new: it stresses the role of prioritisation, or Triage as Davis calls it.

This cheerful word from military/disaster medicine is adopted by Davis because, he says, it perfectly suits the requirements situation.

Triage means dividing casualties into three groups: those that'll certainly die; those that'll certainly get better; and those that need treatment more or less urgently to survive.

Some things are no-brainers; there's no need for discussion. Some are hopelessly dreamy: they should instantly be dismissed. And the rest need to be ranked in a practical way so you can plan your projects.

These correspond to Davis' view of the requirements process: Elicitation, Triage, and Specification.
