     Elements of a Product Vision

1. Where are we heading?
   Problems, goals, markets,
   beneficiaries

2. How do we go there?
   Main functions, features, constraints

3. Why will it succeed?
   Milestones, risks, approach

             Customers’ rights
As a customer, you can expect that
  – Analysts speak your language
  – Learn your domain, your goals
  – Document your requirements and formalize them
  – Explanations for important decision will be shared
    with you
  – You will be involved in the production of ideas, dome
    decisions
  – Have an opportunity to share your priorities,
    concerns,
       Customers’ responbilities
As a customer you have the responsibility to :
   – Train analysts in your domain
   – Clarify terminology
   – Be precise, explicit on your demands, priorities,
     concerns
   – Respect others demands, priorities, concerns
   – Provide insightful feedback when asked, eg. By
     reviewing documents, participating to evaluations, etc
   – Comply with the project management rules and
     organisation
